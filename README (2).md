## About

I'm a Computer Science student at McPherson University with First Class standing,
working toward becoming a machine learning engineer. I don't have a fixed
specialization — I move across tabular ML, computer vision, forecasting, and NLP
depending on the problem, because the goal is engineering judgment, not a
narrow toolset.

I care less about running a notebook that "works once" and more about
understanding why a model behaves the way it does, and being able to explain
that to someone else. Most of what I build ends up either deployed somewhere
people can actually click on, or written up so the next person (usually future
me) doesn't have to re-derive it.

Outside of coursework, I lead PyClub McPherson (100+ members, 20+ workshops
run since February 2025), serve as DSN Campus Ambassador, and am President of
NACOS at McPherson.

## How I work

- Understand the model before deploying it. If I can't explain a result,
  I don't trust it yet.
- Build for zero-cost first. Most of my projects run entirely on free-tier
  infrastructure by design, not by accident.
- Log the process, not just the outcome — including the runs that didn't
  work, because those are usually where the actual learning happened.
